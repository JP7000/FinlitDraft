How we began

We were students before we were founders.

Each of us came to financial literacy from a different direction. One of us grew up watching a family navigate money decisions without ever being taught how. Another sat through a personal finance unit that felt rushed, outdated, and disconnected from real life. A third simply asked a question none of us could answer: why does something this important feel so hard to learn?

We started comparing notes — about the classes we'd taken, the ones we hadn't, the friends who felt just as lost as we did. What we found was a pattern. Financial education wasn't missing because young people didn't care. It was missing because it had never been built for us in the first place — confusing when it should have been clear, and available to some schools while entirely absent from others.

That realization became a decision. In 2022, we founded Futures Financials — not as an idea handed down from adults, but as something built by students who'd lived the problem and wanted to solve it together. What began as a handful of conversations between people from very different backgrounds grew into a shared belief that still drives everything we do: every young person deserves the knowledge and confidence to make informed financial decisions, regardless of where they come from.

Financial confidence starts with financial knowledge.

— The belief behind everything we build

Community at its core

A model built by community, for community.

Our chapters are led by students who know their own communities best. That's what makes our approach to financial education accessible, engaging, and culturally relevant no matter where a chapter is based — because the people teaching it have actually lived there.

Our volunteers don't just teach — they learn continuously from one another, trading ideas across chapters and countries. Every workshop makes the next one better. In the process, our volunteers grow into stronger educators, more capable leaders, and financially empowered global citizens themselves.

Our why

Reimagining how young people learn about money

Founded in 2022, Futures Financials was created to reimagine how young people learn about money. We believe financial literacy should be practical, engaging, and available to everyone — not determined by where a student happens to live or go to school.

Our what

Making financial literacy engaging and practical

We bring financial education to life through hands-on, real-world learning rather than lectures and worksheets.

  • Interactive workshops
  • Educational resources
  • Student mentorship
  • Real-world financial learning

A global network of student volunteers
